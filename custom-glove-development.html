Custom glove development, from brief to production.

For bike, golf, gym and outdoor brands, share a glove sample, artwork or technical pack. GRIPORA plans OEM prototypes, custom materials, branding and sample development around your market and target quantity.

A clear route from idea to approved glove.

Each project is developed around the intended use, target price, fit and brand requirements. The specification is confirmed before sampling so construction, materials and artwork stay aligned.

01 · BRIEF

Share your requirement

Send the glove category, reference images or sample, artwork, target quantity, market and any required performance details.

02 · SPECIFICATION

Confirm construction

We review material, palm, padding, reinforcement, grip, cuff, sizing, branding and packaging options with you.

03 · SAMPLE

Develop and refine

A sample is prepared for evaluation. Fit, materials, artwork and construction are refined before production approval.

Approved specification to export production.

Sample development is typically around 14 days. After sample and specification approval, approximate bulk production lead time is 60 days. The buyer covers sample courier or freight; final MOQ and lead time depend on the selected materials and customization.
