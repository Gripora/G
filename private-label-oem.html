PRIVATE LABEL · OEM · SIALKOT, PAKISTAN

Private-label and OEM gloves for performance brands.

GRIPORA helps brands, distributors and product teams develop golf, cycling, MTB, fitness and winter gloves with custom materials, construction, colors, logos, labels and packaging.

A manufacturing program built around your brand.

Use an existing construction as a starting point or develop a glove around your target market, reference sample and technical brief. We coordinate the details from first specification through approved sample and export production.

01 · PRODUCT

Category and construction

Choose full-finger MTB, golf, fitness or winter gloves, then define fit, panels, cuff and performance requirements.

02 · MATERIALS

Materials and performance

Select suitable backhand, palm, lining, padding, reinforcement, grip and touchscreen options for the intended use.

03 · BRAND

Private-label identity

Develop custom colors, artwork, logos, woven or printed labels and retail packaging around your brand guidelines.

Flexible for trial orders and growing programs.

Starting MOQ is 50 pairs per design, subject to materials and customization. Sample development is typically around 14 days, and bulk production is approximately 60 days after approval.
