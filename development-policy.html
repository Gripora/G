01 / DEVELOPMENT STEPS

FROM IDEA TO PRODUCTION.

A practical six-stage approach. Exact quantities, pricing, timelines and delivery arrangements are discussed and confirmed separately for each project.

01

SHARE YOUR BRIEF

Send product photos, an existing glove, artwork or technical pack. Include quantity, intended use, size range, target price and branding requirements where possible.

02

TECHNICAL REVIEW

Review the proposed construction, palm materials, print options, fit, cuff, trim, branding and feasibility of the requested specifications.

03

SAMPLE DEVELOPMENT

Confirm development scope, charges if applicable, freight arrangements and delivery schedule. Produce samples against the approved brief.

04

REVIEW & APPROVAL

Inspect fit, construction and appearance. Agree adjustments before sample approval and subsequent production.

05

BULK MANUFACTURING

Begin production following approved specification, order acceptance and mutually agreed commercial terms.

06

PACKING & EXPORT

Prepare approved packing, shipping arrangements and the documents required for the destination.

03 / PRACTICAL ANSWERS

FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Is the starting MOQ fixed for every design?

No. The 50-pair quantity is an indicative starting reference from the current website. Final MOQ depends on design, materials, sourcing and customization.

Is sampling completed within 14 days?

Approximately 14 days is an indicative target. Material availability, design complexity, revisions and shipping can affect timing. Confirm the schedule per project.

Is production always completed in 60 days?

Approximately 60 days is a reference, not a fixed guarantee. Confirm manufacturing schedules after sample approval and order acceptance.

Can we supply our own logo or tech pack?

Yes. Share your artwork, construction requirements or reference. GRIPORA can review suitable private-label options and the proposed development approach.

How are sample charges, shipping and payments handled?

These terms are quoted and agreed separately for each project before work proceeds. No universal pricing or payment policy is implied.
